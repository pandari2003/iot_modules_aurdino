1. Main controller boards


Component	Quantity	Purpose
ESP32 development board	1	Transmitter/remote controller
Arduino UNO/Nano	1	Receiver and servo/motor controller
E01-ML01DP5 NRF24L01P wireless module	2	Wireless communication


2. Transmitter controls


Component	Quantity	Purpose
2-axis joystick module	2	AB and CD joystick control
10 kΩ potentiometer	2	Servo P1 and P2 control
Push button	1	Switch Servo/Driving mode
LED	1	Mode indicator
220 Ω resistor	1	LED current limiting


3. Receiver/mechanical components


Component	Quantity	Purpose
Standard hobby servo	6	AY1, BX1, P1, CY2, DX2, P2
DRV8833 motor driver	1	Controls two DC motors
DC geared motor	2	Left and right drive motors
Robot/car chassis	1	Mechanical platform
Wheels	2 or 4	Depending on your chassis
Caster wheel	1	If using a 2-wheel-drive chassis


4. Power

I recommend separate power supplies for the motors/servos and logic.

Component	Quantity	Purpose
Battery pack for DC motors	1	Motor power
Suitable 5–6 V supply/BEC for servos	1	Six servo power
5 V USB/power supply or regulator	1	Arduino/logic if needed
3.3 V regulated supply	1	E01 modules if your board doesn't provide a suitable supply
Capacitors, e.g. 470–1000 µF	1–2	Reduce servo/motor supply voltage dips
